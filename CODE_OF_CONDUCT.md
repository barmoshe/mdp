# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and considerate. Harassment, insults, and other
unwelcome behavior are not acceptable in any project space, public or private.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to
Barprojectsandbuilds@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may take any action they consider appropriate, up to and including a ban.

The full standards and enforcement guidelines are at the link above.
